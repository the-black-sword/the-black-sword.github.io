No new star

No new star. Sixty-two stars in my frame were matched one for one with the survey, and nothing in the galaxy or the field is left over except two specks at the level of the background noise.

The survey’s plate is burned out at the bright centre of the galaxy, so the core itself cannot be checked this way. The long thin line in the survey image is a satellite or aircraft trail on the original plate, not something in the sky tonight.
