How the verdict is reached

Four readings are scored and averaged. The dew point spread (air temperature minus dew point) matters most for the optics: below about 8°F, glass begins to fog.

Station data: Ambient Weather Network, station BlackSword, refreshed about once a minute. Cloud forecast: Open-Meteo. The Moon is calculated from the date.
