The dusty and extremely red progenitor of the type II supernova 2023ixf in Messier 101

Xiang, Mo, Wang et al. · Science China Physics, Mechanics & Astronomy 67 (2024)

In May 2023 a supernova appeared in the Pinwheel Galaxy, one of the closest in a decade. Because Hubble and Spitzer had photographed M101 for years, astronomers could look back at the star before it died. They found a cool, dust-shrouded red supergiant of about twelve times the Sun’s mass, which seems to have shed material rapidly in its final years, perhaps because of a companion star.
