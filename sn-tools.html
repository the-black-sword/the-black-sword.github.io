The Supernova Hunt

Tools of the Trade

An amateur’s telescope is only half the instrument. The other half is a set of free, professional-grade resources.

  • Aladin LiteAn interactive atlas of the whole sky built from professional surveys. Type a galaxy’s name and compare your own image against archival plates.
  • The Transient Name ServerThe official clearing house of the International Astronomical Union, where new supernovae are reported and given their names.
  • Latest Supernovae (David Bishop)A long-running, readable list of recent supernovae and their brightness, much loved by amateurs.
  • SIMBAD & NEDDatabases of known objects: what a galaxy is, how far away, and what has been found in it before.
  • NASA Astrophysics Data System & arXivWhere the research lives, much of it free to read.
  • AAVSOThe American Association of Variable Star Observers, home of amateur brightness measurements since 1911.
