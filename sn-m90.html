Probably nothing, but worth a second look. Forty-one stars matched the survey one for one.

One faint, star-like point in the field has no partner in the survey. It lies well away from the galaxy, where a supernova would be unlikely. M90 sits close to the ecliptic, the path of the planets, where asteroids are common, and a passing asteroid is the most likely explanation. The next image of M90 will settle it: an asteroid will have moved on.
