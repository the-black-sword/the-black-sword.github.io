No new star. Fifty-six stars matched the survey one for one. Everything left over lies in the bright inner disk of the galaxy, where the survey plate is too saturated to compare.

M81 fills so much of the frame that the stars had to be matched from the edges of the field inward; it was the hardest of the thirteen to align.
