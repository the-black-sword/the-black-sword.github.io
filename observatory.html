The BlackSword Observatory is a citizen-science observatory under the skies of the Sacramento Valley.

It began, as so many observatories do, with one telescope and a curious child. Today it is the astronomy wing of the Dragon’s Integrated Citizen Science Program: a working field observatory whose main work is a patient search for supernovae in nearby galaxies.

Amateurs still matter in astronomy. Professional telescopes cannot watch every galaxy every night, and many supernovae are first noticed by people with modest instruments and a great deal of persistence. This room records that work as it happens.

Wander the room as you did the hall: the telescope, the screens, the charts and the open dome all lead somewhere.
